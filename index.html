Brand Introduction

Rooted in Tradition, Designed for the Present

Rajat Viraasat celebrates India’s rich silversmithing traditions with a modern editorial perspective. Every creation reflects artisanal precision and the quiet poetry of heritage objects passed through generations.

Our Philosophy

We preserve authenticity while embracing contemporary expression—bringing culturally meaningful silver craftsmanship into serene, modern lifestyles.

Heritage & Craftsmanship

Centuries of Silversmithing Excellence

From temple motifs to regal filigree work, our craft is inspired by regional traditions across Rajasthan, Gujarat, and Tamil Nadu. Techniques are practiced by master karigars who shape each detail by hand.

  • Hand-engraving and repoussé precision
  • Traditional motifs with contemporary restraint
  • Ethically curated artisan collaborations
