Brand Story

Legacy preserved for contemporary living

Rajat Viraasat is an informative silver heritage house that documents and showcases India’s master silversmith traditions in a calm, design-forward format.

Our Intent

To honor Indian craftsmanship with modern restraint—without noise, without clutter, and without commerce-led distraction.

Heritage & Craftsmanship

Each narrative reflects hand-engraving, repoussé detailing, temple-inspired motifs, and region-specific craftsmanship from India’s renowned artisan communities.
